Why this portfolio exists

This site has three jobs. The first is to be a working record of my co-op term at Jardine Engineering Corporation — the drawings, calculations, and documentation I actually produced, kept somewhere more durable than a folder on a laptop.

The second is to show an employer how I work rather than only what I've done. A resume can list AutoCAD and technical documentation. It can't show the reasoning behind a decision, the feedback I was given and what I changed because of it, or an objective I set and didn't reach. Those are on this site deliberately, because they're the parts that actually distinguish one second-year student from another.

The third is for me. Writing this forced me to be specific about things I could otherwise have left vague — what I was actually optimising for in May, why work kept coming back to me, and what I want to be reliable enough to be trusted with next. I expect to keep adding to it well past the end of this course.
