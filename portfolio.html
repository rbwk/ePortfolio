CourseSelf-Management

Self-Assessments & Course Participation

The DISC profile, values assessment, About Me assignment, and discussion board contributions completed across the term, summarised rather than posted individually.

What I took from it: The DISC result (S/CD — Supportive primary, Cautious and Dominant secondary) predicted my working style more accurately than I expected: the Cautious trait is visibly the one I had to grow into, since the first half of my term was spent being noticeably less cautious than the profile suggested I was. The values assessment was the opposite — it read as generic when I completed it, and only became useful once I had real situations to test it against. My honest conclusion is that these instruments are close to worthless as predictions and genuinely useful as vocabulary: they gave me words for a pattern I'd otherwise have only felt.

PersonalCreativity & Innovation

Sim Racing Shifter

Designed and built a fully functional sim racing shifter from scratch, using Hall-effect sensors and interchangeable 3D-printed cam profiles. Self-taught CAD and 3D printing to take it from concept to a working product.

The cam profiles are interchangeable because the first one felt wrong in a way I couldn't articulate — it worked, it registered every shift, and it still wasn't right. Iterating on the gate geometry until the throw felt mechanical rather than switch-like is the closest thing I've done to real design work, and it's where I learned that "functional" and "finished" are different standards.

PersonalCreativity & Innovation

VEX Robotics Competition Robot

Independently rebuilt a VEX Robotics competition robot nearly from scratch, redesigning mechanisms and iterating on the build to improve performance for competition.

Competition is an unusually honest form of feedback: a mechanism either survives the match or it doesn't, and there's no arguing with the result. Rebuilding rather than patching taught me to trace a failure back to the design decision that caused it instead of reinforcing whatever broke last.
